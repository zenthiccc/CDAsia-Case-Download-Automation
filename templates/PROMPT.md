below are the instructions on creating the digests for each case

follow the content style and flow of "SAMPLE-DIGEST-STYLE-TO-FOLLOW.md"

Make a case digest for each case based on the syllabus.md or txt file
Make a detailed digest out of the cases/pdfs. 
Case Title (FULL VERBATIM)
Docket Number, Date, Ponente and Division
Topic
Principle of the Case
Keywords
Nature
Recit ready summary
Give me a short, immediately recitable summary containing:
Essential relevant facts
Relevant issue (based on the syllabus topic)
Ruling 
Key reason/doctrine (law then application)
This should be concise enough to use during an actual recitation but complete enough to answer the professor’s initial question.
Detailed Facts (PROVIDE THE COMPLETE FACTS IN BULLET POINT SENTENCES. KEEP IT DETAILED, INCLUDING THE MINOR DETAILS)
Procedural History (Who Filed the case? Include the ruling of trial court. Include their reasoning in full as well.  Who filed the appeal? Include the ruling of CA. Include their reasoning in full as well. Make sure the procedural history is IN ORDER.)
Petitioner’s Arguments (ALL ARGUMENTS raised. List them in sequence.)
Respondent’s Arguments (ALL ARGUMENTS raised. List them in sequence.)
Issue/s (Whether or Not format. Do not say whether or not a certain Court erred, just focus on the substance. Focus on the topic - also make sure u are actually referencing the issue explicitly mentioned in the case, FOLLOW THE EXACT WORDING - if any, as much as possible do not merely infer issues from the case unless theres no explicit issue section) 
Ruling/s (Yes or No. Provide a substantive ruling related to the topic. INCLUDE ALL THE REASONING, ALL CITED JURISPRUDENCE, AND KEEP IMPORTANT VERBATIM PHRASES. Everything you think is important to take note of. then application to facts, expound on the relevant ruling (based on the syllabus topic, and make headers that capture each essential topic being discussed in the relevant ruling)
Doctrines and Legal Basis (Cite all the provisions verbatim. Mention the cases discussed and their ratio summary)
Dispositive (Verbatim)
If and only if there are opinions found, summarize the opinion of each justice.
